AUDIT

Calculation Methodology & Audit Trail

Permanent reference for the preliminary design, vessel sizing, metal-weight and cost-estimating methods used by this app. It does not change with the calculated price.

1. Cylindrical Shell Thickness

The app evaluates both shell stress directions for the selected diameter basis and uses the greater calculated thickness.

  • Inside diameter: longitudinal t = P × Ri / (2 × S × E + 0.4 × P); circumferential t = P × Ri / (S × E − 0.6 × P).
  • Outside diameter: longitudinal t = P × Ro / (2 × S × E + 1.4 × P); circumferential t = P × Ro / (S × E + 0.4 × P).
  • Required nominal thickness = governing pressure thickness + corrosion allowance + forming allowance + other selected allowance.
  • Automatic FEED plate selection: the app selects the next higher value from the VesselM preferred nominal series. For example, 20.6 mm becomes 22 mm. The selection is then used for thickness comparison, weight and cost.
  • Example: if governing pressure thickness is 12 mm, corrosion allowance is 3 mm and forming allowance is 1 mm, preliminary nominal thickness is 16 mm before selection of the next available plate thickness.

2. Vessel Sizing & Internals

Preliminary vessel size is derived from the selected process duty, phases, working capacity, separation needs and applicable API RP 12J process data.

  • For a selected L/D ratio: D = cube root of [4 × V / (π × L/D)].
  • Tangent-line length: L = (L/D) × D.
  • Orientation and internals are screening recommendations only; process retention time, fluid properties, slugging, nozzle sizing and vendor design must be confirmed.
  • Example: V = 20 m³ and L/D = 3 gives D ≈ 2.04 m and tangent-line length ≈ 6.12 m.

3. Head & Cone Thickness Formula Library

These are the fixed pressure-thickness equations implemented for every head and cone option. Use consistent units: P and S in MPa; diameters and radii in mm; resulting thickness t in mm. In every case, add corrosion, forming and other allowances after the pressure thickness.

2:1 Ellipsoidal Head

  • Inside basis: t = P × Di / (2 × S × E − 0.2 × P).
  • Outside basis: t = P × Do / (2 × S × E + 1.8 × P).
  • Example: P = 1 MPa, Di = 2,000 mm, S = 120 MPa, E = 1.00: t = 1 × 2,000 / (240 − 0.2) = 8.34 mm.

General Ellipsoidal Head

  • K = 0.167 × [2 + (Di / 2h)²].
  • Inside basis: t = P × Di × K / (2 × S × E − 0.2 × P).
  • Outside basis: t = P × Do × K / [2 × S × E + 2 × P × (K − 0.1)].
  • Example: P = 1 MPa, Di = 2,000 mm, h = 500 mm, S = 120 MPa, E = 1.00; K ≈ 1.002, therefore t ≈ 8.36 mm.

Hemispherical Head

  • Inside basis: t = P × Ri / (2 × S × E − 0.2 × P).
  • Outside basis: t = P × Ro / (2 × S × E + 0.8 × P).
  • Example: P = 1 MPa, Ri = 1,000 mm, S = 120 MPa, E = 1.00: t = 1,000 / (240 − 0.2) = 4.17 mm.

Torispherical Head, 100-6

  • The app uses L = D and knuckle radius r = 0.06 × D.
  • Inside basis: t = 0.885 × P × Li / (S × E − 0.1 × P).
  • Outside basis: t = 0.885 × P × Lo / (S × E + 0.8 × P).
  • Example: P = 1 MPa, Li = 2,000 mm, S = 120 MPa, E = 1.00: t = 0.885 × 2,000 / 119.9 = 14.76 mm.

General Torispherical Head

  • M = 0.25 × [3 + √(L / r)].
  • Inside basis: t = P × Li × M / (2 × S × E − 0.2 × P).
  • Outside basis: t = P × Lo × M / [2 × S × E + P × (M − 0.2)].
  • Example: P = 1 MPa, L = 2,000 mm, r = 120 mm, S = 120 MPa, E = 1.00; M ≈ 1.771, therefore t ≈ 14.77 mm.

Conical Section

  • α is the cone half-apex angle. The app calculates both longitudinal and circumferential cases, then uses the greater value.
  • Inside basis: longitudinal t = P × Di / [4 × cos α × (S × E + 0.4 × P)]; circumferential t = P × Di / [2 × cos α × (S × E − 0.6 × P)].
  • Outside basis: longitudinal t = P × Do / [4 × cos α × (S × E + 1.4 × P)]; circumferential t = P × Do / [2 × cos α × (S × E + 0.4 × P)].
  • Example: P = 1 MPa, Di = 2,000 mm, α = 30°, S = 120 MPa, E = 1.00: longitudinal t ≈ 4.80 mm and circumferential t ≈ 9.68 mm governing.

4. Preliminary Metal Weight

Shell weight: Wshell = π × D × L × t × ρ.

  • D = vessel diameter in m; L = tangent-line length in m; t = nominal shell thickness in m.
  • ρ = selected steel density, normally 7,850 kg/m³ for preliminary steel weight.
  • The selected head or cone is included using its geometry-area approximation and nominal thickness.
  • Liquid-boot applicability: this FEED allowance is applied only when installation is aboveground, orientation is horizontal, and Liquid boot required is selected.
  • Boot dimensions: vessel ID below 900 mm → 300 mm boot ID; 900–1,400 mm → 400 mm boot ID; above 1,400 mm → 500 mm boot ID. Boot straight length = max(1,000 mm, 3 × boot ID), rounded up to the next 100 mm. This length ends at the bottom-head tangent line; it excludes the bottom-head depth.
  • Boot metal-weight build-up: Wboot basic = (π × Dboot × Lboot × t × ρ) + (A2:1 ellipsoidal bottom head × t × ρ). The app uses the vessel shell nominal thickness for the boot shell and bottom head.
  • Underground submersible-pump nozzle: when selected, use a 24 in NB vessel nozzle/riser with 14 mm wall and 2.0 m minimum projection from vessel shell to end point. Add 20% of riser weight for the 150 lb top flange and attachment allowance.
  • Attachment allowance: Wboot total = Wboot basic × 1.15, which includes 15% for attachment details.
  • Ladder and platform allowance, when selected: WL&P = 5% × bare vessel weight, including the boot allowance when present.
  • Example: for a 2,000 mm vessel ID and 16 mm shell, the app selects a 500 mm ID × 1,500 mm straight boot. Including its 2:1 ellipsoidal bottom head and 15% attachments, the boot allowance is approximately 0.38 MT.
FEED estimate control

Boot process volume, interface control, drainage, final wall thickness, reinforcement, support/attachment details and mechanical design require process and mechanical verification. This allowance is not permitted for construction use.

4A. Installed Mixer Weight

For a selected agitation / mixing requirement, the app uses motor power first; vessel capacity is only a fallback.

  • Power basis: 0–7.5 kW = 450 kg; >7.5–15 = 700 kg; >15–30 = 1,100 kg; >30–55 = 1,800 kg; >55–90 = 2,800 kg; >90–130 = 4,000 kg.
  • Volume fallback: up to 5 m³ = 450 kg; >5–15 = 700 kg; >15–40 = 1,100 kg; >40–80 = 1,800 kg; >80–150 = 2,800 kg; above 150 m³ = 4,000 kg and manual review.
  • Adjustment: base installed weight × selected duty factor. For a top-entry mixer, use the greater of ×1.10 or +200 kg. Apply a 400 kg minimum and round up to the next 50 kg.
  • For a horizontal vessel, side-entry is the default arrangement. The allowance includes package and mounting weight only; it excludes maintenance platform, cabling, piping, pump, liquid, dynamic loads and nozzle-load verification.
FEED estimate control

Vendor confirmation and mechanical review are required. This allowance is not for construction use.

4B. Automatic Skirt / Saddle Allowance

The app selects a preliminary support from vessel orientation: one skirt for a vertical vessel, or two saddles (one fixed and one sliding) for a horizontal vessel.

  • Horizontal saddle locations: each saddle is placed from the nearest tangent line by the lesser of 20% of tangent-line length or 0.5 × vessel diameter, rounded to 50 mm.
  • 4 m check: a calculated saddle spacing above 4 m is shown as a FEED review message. It is not a final-design failure and does not automatically create extra saddles.
  • Weight: the estimate includes preliminary plates, wear plate, web/base details and an attachments allowance. This weight is added to the total metal weight and cost estimate.
  • Final support location, foundation, anchor bolts, wind, seismic, local shell stress, lifting and transport checks remain for detailed engineering.
FEED estimate control

This automatic allowance is for budget estimating only. It is not permitted for site installation or fabrication use.

4. Material Rate & Supply Price

Material rate: Rmaterial = RCS,year × Fmaterial.

  • RCS,year is the selected raw carbon-steel rate for the chosen year.
  • Fmaterial is the selected material-family factor relative to carbon steel.
  • Supply P50: raw material value × 1.80. Supply P90: raw material value × 2.70.
  • Example: 10,000 kg × ₹56.50/kg = ₹5.65 lakh raw material value; P50 supply = ₹10.17 lakh and P90 supply = ₹15.26 lakh.

5. Site Erection & Total Cost

P50 erection cost: vessel operating weight (MT) × applicable P50 erection rate (₹/MT). P90 erection cost: P50 erection cost × 1.50.

  • The app selects the single-piece P50 benchmark band by vessel weight, or the multi-piece P50 benchmark when selected.
  • P90 management provision: 50% above the calculated P50 erection cost (P90 = P50 × 1.50).
  • Total P50: P50 supply price + P50 erection cost.
  • Total P90: P90 supply price + P90 erection cost.
  • Example: 20 MT × ₹35,450/MT = ₹7.09 lakh P50 erection cost; P90 erection = ₹7.09 lakh × 1.50 = ₹10.64 lakh.

6. Audit Limits & Engineering Controls

  • All calculations are preliminary engineering and FEED estimating tools, not final code design or vendor fabrication calculations.
  • Allowable stress, code edition, material compatibility, corrosion, weld efficiency, nozzle loads, wind, seismic, lifting and fatigue require project verification.
  • Automatic weight includes a FEED-only skirt or saddle allowance based on vessel orientation. It excludes detailed support design, foundation, anchors, nozzles, internals, insulation, paint and fabrication wastage unless separately allowed.
  • Total cost excludes civil foundation, utilities, E&I hook-up, commissioning, taxes, escalation and project-specific site constraints.
  • User-entered vessel weight is used for the quick cost estimate in place of automatic weight.

ENGINEERING GOVERNANCE

Basis, Controls and Limitations

Use the app as a transparent preliminary workflow, not as an autonomous design authority.

A

Source-derived

Material ranges and component specifications follow Appendix H. Internal-pressure formulas follow the historical manual’s procedures.

B

User-controlled

Code edition, joint efficiency, geometry and thickness additions are entered by the user. The app selects the FEED nominal plate thickness from the controlled VesselM library. Allowable stress comes only from the included limited plate library.

C

Not included

External pressure, MDMT, PWHT, nozzles, fatigue, local loads, final support/foundation design, lifting, transportation and detailed integrity assessment.

Phase 6 activation conditions

  1. Approve the controlled code edition and addenda.
  2. Verify each enabled formula and applicability limit.
  3. Select an exact supported plate record with a published library value, or add a controlled verified record before calculation.
  4. Approve joint-efficiency and hydrotest logic.
  5. Complete independent calculations and retain test evidence.
